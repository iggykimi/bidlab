# BidLab 180s video — English narration script (V6, 30-oct-2026)

Title: "Muse Spark + BidLab: the Meta integration in action".
Total: <= 180 s. Pace: ~130 wpm. Screen: 1280x720, real app at the
V6 HEAD, mouse visible. No mock calls on camera.

## S1 hook (0:00–0:20, ~40 words)

"Would you give up five thousand nine hundred euros for the same
price score? This is BidLab, a local-first bidding assistant.
A ten percent discount scores forty-nine out of forty-nine and
keeps sixteen thousand five hundred euros of profit. Fifteen
percent scores the same — with ten thousand five hundred."

## S2 evidence trail (0:20–0:50, ~60 words)

"Every figure carries its source. This is a real Spanish tender,
Tias twenty twenty-six, with one hundred eighteen thousand euros
of budget. Points, prices, and citations come from reviewed rules,
never from guesses. Unknown stays unknown — the app will not
invent a zero. When something needs a human, it says so, in plain
language, before you decide."

## S3 curve and trials (0:50–1:20, ~60 words)

"The curve shows your points for every price. I set a ten percent
discount: one hundred six thousand five hundred euros, forty-nine
points. I save it as a trial. Then fifteen percent: one hundred
thousand five hundred, still forty-nine points. Same score, less
profit. Both trials are saved with their full context, ready to
compare."

## S4 compare and frontier (1:20–1:50, ~60 words)

"Now I compare both trials side by side. The table shows price,
profit, and points for each. The frontier answers which trial
survives: ten percent dominates, because nothing else reaches its
points and profit and improves at least one of the two. The duel
at the bottom spells out the difference: five thousand nine
hundred sixteen euros of profit, with identical points."

## S5 report and export (1:50–2:20, ~55 words)

"I open the ten percent trial and create a decision report: prepare
the offer, with my own note. Pending checks stay visible until a
human clears them. The technical details keep the rule, the company
revision, and the snapshot hash. One click downloads the same report
as a printable document, in Spanish, with every figure sourced."

## S6 Meta live evidence (2:16–2:50, recorded 33.6 s)

"This is the review flow. Nothing is ever sent to Meta without
consent, a key, and a budget. Here, everything stays manual. The
same pipeline extracted twelve synthetic adversarial cases through
Muse Spark: scanned pages, tables, contradictions, lots, and an
injection attack — ignored. Eighteen out of eighteen figures
verified against the documents, zero critical errors, for about
one cent. Evidence, not promises."

(First take said "This is the opt-in Meta flow" over the company
form; re-recorded to match the visible review screen. No consent
checkbox is shown because the demo server has no live service —
nothing is faked.)

## S7 close (2:50–3:00, ~20 words)

"BidLab: honest numbers for public tenders, with Meta inside where
it counts. Thank you."
